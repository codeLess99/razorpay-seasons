# Razorpay Seasons: user journey

Simulated date in the prototype: Mon, 7 Sep 2026. Main merchant: Mithas Foods (D2C sweets, Diwali peak).

1. **The merchant hears about it on WhatsApp.** RAY sends: "Diwali is on 8 Nov this year. Expect the rush from ~18 Oct. 4 actions are ready for your approval." Tapping it opens Seasons in the dashboard.
2. **Overview.** The next moment and a countdown ("41 days to your peak"), Quick insights (what Seasons did and what the merchant could do next), what data Seasons is using for each layer, three layer cards (week, month, year), the upcoming timeline, and how Seasons works.
3. **Year: the season plan.** A chart of the merchant's year with the forecast for the coming peak. Seasons uses the merchant's own ramp ("orders rose 21 days before Diwali last year"), not a fixed calendar, because Diwali moves. Four actions, each with why, impact, confidence, timing and the Razorpay product it runs on (ⓘ explains each product and whether it exists today).
4. **Review & approve.** The merchant opens an action, sees exactly who gets the message (customers without WhatsApp opt-in are excluded), edits the text, previews it on WhatsApp, and approves. For setting or money actions they see a before/after table; money decisions are only prefilled, never submitted.
5. **Month: ask at the right time.** For Kata Martial Arts Academy, fee links go out when each family usually pays (most right after salary day) on WhatsApp instead of SMS on the 28th. Reliable families are offered UPI AutoPay. Removing class access waits for the merchant's approval.
6. **Week: regulars and quiet slots.** For The Courtside Club, Seasons spots six Friday regulars who have gone quiet and drafts personal check-ins, and suggests a members-only offer for empty Tuesday evenings, within the discount limit.
7. **New merchant: predictive mode.** For Thanda Cool Appliances (21 days on Razorpay), Seasons predicts from 240 similar merchants and shows when each layer switches to the merchant's own data.
8. **Review & learn.** After a season, Seasons compares forecast with actual results, shows what each action did, and asks the merchant what it couldn't see ("we ran out of kaju katli by day 4"). The notes become proposed changes for the next plan, and the merchant chooses which to keep.
9. **Controls.** Seasons' commitments (merchant approves every customer message; only opted-in customers; every step logged) plus the merchant's preferences: discount limit, messages per month, quiet hours, SMS backup, which layers run, and pause. Loosening a protection asks for confirmation.
10. **Activity log.** Every suggestion, approval, edit and change, with who did it.

Use **Take the tour** in the app for a guided version of this journey.
